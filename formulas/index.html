Every formula, published

Basalt: Health & Fitness · reflecting the app's constants as of 9 September 2026

Every number Basalt shows you is computed by a rule you can read. The in-app math sheets show each rule applied to your own numbers; this page lists the rules themselves. If a rule changes, this page changes with it, dated. Nothing here is aspirational — if the app can't do it yet, it isn't listed.

Energy & targets

  • Resting energy — Mifflin-St Jeor BMR from your age, sex, height and weight.
  • Activity — earned from your own last 21 days of steps and logged sessions (needs ≥14 days with steps before it's personal); active energy is modelled from step count and session work, with walking cadence handled so a walk isn't counted twice. The resulting factor is banded ±20% — never a single false-precise multiplier.
  • Targets are ranges — BMR × activity, with your goal delta applied, produces a calorie band with its mid shown (worked example in-app: 2,043–2,597 kcal, mid 2,320). Protein, carbs, fat and caps follow from it.
  • Rails — a hard floor of 1,200 kcal and a sex-aware floor of 1,500; a goal that outpaces a safe rate is met with a plain "slow down", never a silent clamp; under-18 is gated out of aggressive deficits. No target is set from a BMI shortcut.
  • Water goal — weight kg × 32 ml (+ activity and goal adjustments); 2,200 ml fallback without a weight.
  • Fill-the-gap — nothing renders under 150 kcal remaining; a food that exceeds the remaining energy is excluded outright; your own foods fill slots before Open Food Facts does.

The programme generator

Deterministic — the same intake always yields the same programme, and the rules are these:

  • Split by days — your available training days pick the template (full-body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs…), drawn from a fixed catalogue of movements matched to your equipment.
  • Limitations cap difficulty — each limitation you note (e.g. knee, shoulder, lower back) caps the difficulty of the movement patterns it affects, substituting gentler variants rather than dropping the pattern.
  • Sets & reps by experience — new lifters get higher-rep, movement-learning ranges and more explanation; intermediate hypertrophy and strength ranges differ by goal.
  • Starting loads — dumbbell work starts at a fraction of your stated capacity (0.5 / 0.4 / 0.3 by experience); barbell lifts start from a percentage of bodyweight; bodyweight-only movements progress by reps and say so.
  • Rest — 150 s for primary lifts, 75 s for accessories.
  • Volume check — the week is checked against the 10–20 hard-set band per muscle; too-low regions gain an accessory (never a new movement, capped at 4 sets, never for new lifters); too-high regions drop from accessories first, then secondaries, down to a floor of 3; anything that still can't be fixed is stated plainly, not hidden.

Progression & training

  • Hard set — a set at RIR ≤ 3 (an unknown RIR counts as hard); this is what the weekly-volume band and the recovery window count.
  • Rep range — 8–12 reps; load moves in 2.5 kg steps.
  • Weekly volume band — 10–20 hard sets per muscle per week (deload halves it); primary sets count 1, secondary 0.5; warm-ups excluded. A change is only proposed when the band is missed two weeks running — never on a single week.
  • Layoff ramp — ≥28 days away → suggest ×0.8 · ≥14 days away → suggest ×0.9.
  • Mesocycle — 3 weeks accumulation · 2 intensification · 1 deload.
  • Early-deload triggers (any two advise) — ≥40% "too hard" feedback in 7 days · 7-day readiness mean < 45 · a main lift stalled 2+ weeks.
  • Training max — TM = 90% of best e1RM over 8 weeks, rounded to 2.5 kg; moves only by ≥2.5 kg. Phase percentages: accumulation 70/72.5/75% · intensification 82.5/85% · deload 60%.
  • Pain flags — you can flag pain on any set; the app never concludes from it, but if a movement is flagged 3 times in 14 days it prompts a substitution.
  • Timer floors — no timed transition shorter than 10 s (user-initiated starts exempt); the tempo metronome is 3-1-1, haptic, off by default.

Readiness

  • Components — HRV vs your 30-day median · resting HR vs median · sleep vs target · training load vs your own P75 — equal weight, each shown with its literal math.
  • Baseline floor — 7 days of a signal before it scores; fewer than 3 scorable components → no score at all.
  • Ratio clamp — each component ratio clamps to [0.6, 1.4] before scoring.
  • Rest advice — readiness below 40 marks a rest-advised day (feeds the rest-aware streak).
  • No wearable required — readiness uses whatever components you actually have; with too few, there is simply no number, stated as such.

Sleep

  • Personal need — median of your last 28 nights, clamped 7:00–10:00; published default 8:00 until 14 nights exist.
  • Strain — a P75-heavy training day adds 30 min to that night's need.
  • Nap credit — a day's longest session is the night; extras ≤ 3:00 h are naps and credit the day; naps never enter the need median.
  • Debt — Σ(need − slept) over 14 nights, floored at zero; surplus repays; absent nights are absent.
  • Bedtime window — target = need + min(1:00, debt ÷ 3), anchored to your median wake; a 30-min window; hidden until the need is personal and 7 wakes exist.
  • Consistency — ±(median absolute deviation) of the last 14 bedtimes around their median.
  • Stages — display-only, by law — stages never enter any score or suggestion.

Nutrition planning

  • Eat-out estimates — a restaurant meal you didn't weigh is logged as a range, not a point: roughly ±25% on a dish you named, ±35% when you're guessing.
  • Batch-cook weeks — a cooked batch divides across the days you assign it, each day carrying its share.
  • Plan adherence — measured against your own plan, stated as a fact, never scored against a stranger's diet.
  • AI estimates — always ranges (~low–high) from the model itself, point inside the range; nothing auto-commits; the eval harnesses that hold the AI to this are committed in the repo.

Signals & trends

  • Graded uncertainty — a measured value renders as a solid line; an inferred or unconfirmed one renders dashed or banded, and an AI value keeps its ~ until you confirm it. The chart never lets a guess look like a measurement.
  • Correlation gates — shown only at |r| ≥ 0.45 with n ≥ 30 days; checked-but-not-shown pairs are listed in-app.
  • Vitals deviation — outside your own 30-day min–max; withheld under 7 baseline days; a card only at ≥2 vitals deviating — never diagnosis language.
  • Streak window — 180 days; program rest days and rest-advised days maintain a run, never start one.
  • Cycle estimate — next-period window = median of your last ≤6 cycles ± your own spread (floor ±2 days); nothing under 2 complete cycles; no phase-based training advice, ever.
  • Co-op — the only shared datum: one boolean per day over 14 days — "logged anything real".

Coaching & check-ins

  • Weekly check-in priority — the week is read in a fixed order: safety, then adherence, then energy, then volume. It states the facts and asks exactly one question. Illness you note never counts against adherence.
  • Pebble Coach — answers are built only from your own numbers and list which ones they used; it proposes at most one action and never edits anything; medical, dosing and disordered-eating questions get a referral, not an answer.
  • Detail levels — Simple, Standard and Full change what is shown, never what is computed: every level runs the same engines on the same numbers, and a conformance test pins that each level's numbers are a subset of the next. Nothing is ever hidden by more than one tap.

Movement

  • Race prediction — Riegel (1977): T2 = T1 × (D2/D1)^1.06; easy pace ×1.3, steady ×1.12 of race pace; plans 6–16 weeks.
  • Route nudge — one buzz beyond 50 m off a chosen loop, re-arming inside 25 m.
