Coming soon to Google Play
One ledger

Everything you track lives in one honest place.

Most apps split your health into four disconnected trackers. Basalt keeps food, training, sleep and vitals in one ledger, so the picture is whole — not four half-pictures that never talk to each other.

Food

Barcode scans, manual entries, recipes and a grocery list — every gram from a real source, sources named on every synced datum.

Training

Sets, reps, weight and RIR logged as you lift. Deterministic progression suggestions from your own history — never a mandate.

Sleep

Sessions and stages, read from Health Connect when you connect it, always labeled with where the number came from.

Vitals

Weight, steps, water, mindfulness — the rest of the picture, real-or-hidden. A gap stays a quiet gap, never a fabricated chart.

The honesty rules

Product law, not a style preference.

These aren't marketing lines. They're constraints the app is built to — every screen is checked against them before it ships.

No fake data
A missing number is a quiet empty state. It is never a placeholder zero, never an invented chart.
Real or hidden
No guilt mechanics
No rings, no streak-breaking shame, no cheerleading copy. Going over a target is stated plainly — never scolded.
Stated, not scored
Sources, always
Every synced value names where it came from — Health Connect, a scan, your own entry. AI estimates wear a visible ~ until you confirm them.
Named on every row
Your data, exportable
Every table, every row you own — as JSON or CSV, in one tap, whenever you want it.
One tap out
Your data, deletable
A real full-cascade delete — every row, then the sign-in record. Not a soft-delete, not a 30-day queue.
Actually gone
No ads, no data sales
Nothing about your health is ever sold or used to advertise to you. There is no analytics SDK in this app.
Not for sale
