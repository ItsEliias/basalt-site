One ledger

Everything you track lives in one honest place.

Most apps split your health into four disconnected trackers. Basalt keeps food, training, sleep and vitals in one ledger, so the picture is whole — not four half-pictures that never talk to each other.

Food

Barcode, photo, voice, a drag-a-plate builder, or plain typing — every gram from a real source, and manual entry is always enough. Capture is never paywalled.

Training

Sets, reps, weight and RIR logged as you lift, with a rest timer and plate maths. A deterministic programme generator builds your split from your own equipment and week.

Sleep & recovery

Sessions, readiness, and a wind-down — read from Health Connect when you connect it, always labelled with where the number came from. Sleep stages are display-only, by law.

Body & mind

Weight, steps, water, walks, a private journal and a words-not-scores mood check-in — the rest of the picture, real-or-hidden. A gap stays a quiet gap, never a fabricated chart.

More than a tracker

It does what a coach does — from your own numbers, with the maths shown.

A ledger records. Basalt also plans, and it shows the arithmetic behind every plan. Nothing is a black box: if a target moved, the reason is one tap away, and the rule is on the formulas page.

A programme built for you
Answer a short PT intake — experience, days, minutes, equipment, any limitations — and Basalt generates a split with sets, reps, rest and starting loads. Knee pain caps squat difficulty; bodyweight-only progresses by reps; an unfixable gap is stated, never hidden.
Rules published
Targets that are ranges, not false precision
BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) × activity earned from your own step and session history, banded ±20%. Rate rails with floors, and a plain "slow down" when a goal outpaces them.
Every number a band
A weekly check-in that states the facts and asks once
It reads your week by a published priority — safety, then adherence, then energy, then volume — states what happened, and asks exactly one question. Illness never counts against adherence.
One ask, not a lecture
Meal plans and a grocery list
Recipe import, ate-out estimates as ranges, batch-cook weeks, and adherence measured against your own plan — not a stranger's diet.
Your plan, your numbers
Yours to shape

The app bends to you, not the reverse.

Everything beyond the core ledger is off by default. With every option untouched, Basalt is exactly the honest ledger above. Turn on only what you want.

Eleven themes

From stern Minimal to glassy Depth to bouncy Gummy. Every colour pair is contrast-verified in the build, so the expressive ones are exactly as legible as the plain ones.

Minimal Depth Gummy

Three detail levels

Simple ("just tell me what to do"), Standard ("show me the numbers"), Full ("show me the maths"). The rule: shown, never computed — every level runs the same engines, and anything hidden is one tap away.

Extras, all off by default

Widgets, meal planning, photos, connected services, motivation surfaces — each a single switch, each honest inside. Nothing is on until you turn it on.

Motion that respects you

A calm motion layer with a personality per theme — and a full reduced-motion path, so Android's "Remove animations" turns all of it off. Meaning is never carried by motion alone.

Pebble

An optional companion that proposes, and never comments.

Pebble is off by default. When it's on, every message is a proposal with an action and a way to dismiss it — built only from your own numbers. It never reviews your day or tells you how you did.

Ask about your own numbers
Pebble Coach answers questions on your Today screen — and lists exactly which of your numbers it used under every answer. It proposes at most one action and never edits anything.
Cites its sources
Hard limits, honoured
Medical, dosing and disordered-eating questions get a referral to a professional, not an answer. Those deserve a person.
Knows its edges
A crisis path that can't be switched off
On any free-text field, a crisis check runs first — entirely on your device, before anything is sent anywhere. It shows regional help lines, and nothing about it ever leaves your phone.
On-device, always on
The honesty rules

Product law, not a style preference.

These aren't marketing lines. They're constraints the app is built to — every screen is checked against them before it ships, and this site follows the same rule.

No fake data
A missing number is a quiet empty state. It is never a placeholder zero, never an invented chart.
Real or hidden
No guilt mechanics
No rings, no streak-breaking shame, no cheerleading copy. Going over a target is stated plainly — never scolded.
Stated, not scored
AI proposes, never narrates
Every AI estimate is a range you can edit, unconfirmed until you say so — and no AI summary ever replaces your own data. The evaluation harnesses that hold it to this are committed in the repo.
A proposal, not a verdict
Sources, always
Every synced value names where it came from — Health Connect, a scan, your own entry. Uncertain values wear a visible ~ and render as bands, not solid lines.
Named on every row
Your data, exportable and deletable
Every table row you own — as JSON, CSV or a per-table archive, in one tap. Deletion is a real full cascade: every row, then the sign-in record. Not a soft-delete, not a 30-day queue.
Yours, fully
No ads, no data sales
Nothing about your health is ever sold or used to advertise to you. There is no analytics SDK in this app.
Not for sale
